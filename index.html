// 06 — Our Commitments

Built On
The Right Values.

BMX Freestyle European Series is built to leave something behind in every city — not just for three days, but for the community that hosts us.

01
Equal Prize Money
Women's and Men's categories compete for identical prize money and identical visibility — on the park, in broadcast, and in every announcement.
02
Open To All
Every host venue is selected and built for accessibility — dedicated access, clear sightlines and support for spectators with disabilities.
03
Built To Be Reused
Our modular park is designed for reuse across the season, not single-use waste. Materials, signage and structures travel from city to city.
04
Local Artists, Main Stage
BMX Series' music and culture programme is built entirely on local talent. In every host city, the DJs, live acts and graffiti artists on site are from that city — not a touring lineup.
// Culture, Not Just Sport

The Stage
Is Yours.

Music and street art are part of every stop — but the lineup isn't ours, it's the city's. Local DJs on the decks, local bands on the stage, local graffiti artists given wall space and full creative freedom on site.

The Series brings the park and the crowd. The city brings the sound and the colour.
