// 05 — Our Commitments

Built On
The Right Values.

BMX Freestyle European Series is built to leave something behind in every city — not just for three days, but for the community that hosts us.

01
Equal Prize Money
Women's and Men's categories compete for identical prize money and identical visibility — on the park, in broadcast, and in every announcement.
02
Open To All
Every host venue is selected and built for accessibility — dedicated access, clear sightlines and support for spectators with disabilities.
03
Built To Be Reused
Our modular park is designed for reuse across the season, not single-use waste. Materials, signage and structures travel from city to city.
04
Local Artists, Main Stage
The Series' music and culture programme is built entirely on local talent. In every host city, the DJs, live acts and graffiti artists on site are from that city — not a touring lineup.

// 06 — Who We Are

The Team
Behind The Series.

BMX Freestyle European Series is developed by Urban Sports Foundation in partnership with Polish Champs Foundation — two organisations with a proven track record in delivering BMX freestyle events at scale.

Together, the organisations have delivered 30+ BMX freestyle events, including more than 20 stops of the Ride The Future series, several editions of the Polish BMX Freestyle Cup, and the Polish BMX Freestyle Championships, organised annually since 2022.

Polish Champs Foundation also delivered the BMX Freestyle Park competition at the 2023 European Games in Kraków — a project requiring close coordination with the UEC, national cycling federations and major sports institutions. This experience has given the team a strong understanding of official competition standards, federation procedures and the operational requirements of large-scale international sporting events.

This experience translates directly into the capabilities required at every Series stop: competition delivery, international athlete coordination, cross-border networks and large-scale event production.
